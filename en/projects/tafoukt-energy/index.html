TAFOUKT ENERGY

A marketing site and a management portal for solar installations.

Overview

A two-phase project for a solar installation company: a public site, then an internal portal to track clients and installations.

What I built

  • Django REST API with 57 files and 5 apps, JWT authentication and roles.
  • Inverter data (Huawei FusionSolar, GoodWe, SMA) fetched on a schedule with Celery.
  • Branded PDF certificates generated with ReportLab.
  • Production charts with Chart.js.
  • A suite of 20 pytest tests.
