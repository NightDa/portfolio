Travol Marrakech

An excursion booking platform in Morocco that I design and evolve on my own.

Overview

Travol Marrakech lets travellers book excursions from Marrakech. I handle everything: architecture, back end, front end, search optimization and the server.

What I built

  • Rebuilt the SEO architecture: centralized canonical, hreflang and schema handling, fixed duplicated meta tags across 100+ pages, restructured the sitemap and redirects.
  • Site in French, English and Spanish, with full translations for 15+ packages.
  • Secure booking: CSRF protection on AJAX requests, double-booking bugs fixed, pricing logic stabilized.
  • White-label booking widget for a partner hotel, with a PHP API, commission tracking and admin reports.
  • Performance: trimmed bundles and self-hosted assets, with a mobile PageSpeed score close to 90.
  • Migration to Next.js, MySQL and Prisma in progress.
